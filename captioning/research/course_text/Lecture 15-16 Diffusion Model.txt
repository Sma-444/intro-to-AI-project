A diffusion model generates new data (such as images) by learning to gradually remove noise from random noise until a realistic sample emerges.

Why are Diffusion Models Popular

What’s Special About Latent Diffusion Model
Pixel-space diffusion
The denoiser processes every image pixel at every sampling step, which is expensive at high resolution.
Latent-space diffusion
A pretrained autoencoder compresses the image   to   in the latent space before diffusion and removes imperceptible detail.
 
H × W × 3 pixels
h × w × c latent
Lower-dimensional tensors reduce compute and memory.
Cross-attention supports flexible text or spatial conditioning.
High-resolution synthesis becomes much more practical.
The autoencoder can introduce reconstruction limitations.

Seven ideas to remember
DIFFUSION MODELS
Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
1
A diffusion model learns to reverse gradual corruption by noise.
2
The forward process is fixed; the reverse process is learned.
3
A common model predicts the added noise at a chosen timestep.
4
U-Nets combine global structure with local detail; DiTs scale with Transformers.
5
Conditioning guides every denoising step.
6
Latent diffusion reduces the cost of high-resolution generation.
7
High quality and flexible control drive popularity; slow sampling and societal risks remain.
