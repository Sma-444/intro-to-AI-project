Common Activation Functions

SLIDE 16
ReLU
Advantages :  It is computationally simple. Compared to other activation functions,  ReLU  requires relatively less computation, and the model training process takes relatively less time.
Disadvantages:  
Dead  ReLU  problem: A  ReLU  neuron may become inactive for essentially all training inputs. Because the  ReLU  gradient is zero for negative inputs, such a neuron may then receive little or no gradient and stop learning .
The derivative does not exist at  x=0.
Sigmoid and  tanh
Advantages:  They  do not suffer from the dead neuron problem and can better control the output range.
Disadvantage:  They may cause the vanishing gradient problem (i.e., during model training, the weight updates become too small, leading to low training efficiency).
`
