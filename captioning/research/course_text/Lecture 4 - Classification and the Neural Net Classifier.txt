SLIDE 26
1. The main difference between a regression problem and a classification problem lies in the type of prediction:
regression predicts numerical quantities;
classification predicts class labels (usually through predicted class probabilities or scores).
2. This difference affects the model output structure. For example, a regression model may output one real number, while a  -class classification model often outputs   scores or probabilities.  
3. The training procedures are often very similar, but they typically use different loss functions. For example:
Regression: MSE;
Classification: cross-entropy.
4. Many other parts of the machine-learning workflow are similar, such  as:data  preprocessing, train/validation/test splitting, optimization, regularization,  hyperparameter  tuning, and model selection.
