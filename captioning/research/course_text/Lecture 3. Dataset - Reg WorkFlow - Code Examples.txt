StratifiedKFold  is a variation of K-Fold cross-validation used for classification tasks.
  In  regular K-Fold , the dataset is split into  K  folds randomly, without considering the distribution of class labels. This may lead to some folds having very few or even no samples of a certain class.
  In  StratifiedKFold , the splitting is done in a  stratified way , which means that each fold preserves the  same class proportion  as in the full dataset.
Why use it?
  Ensures that training and validation sets are representative of the overall dataset.
  Prevents biased performance estimates when the dataset is imbalanced.
  Recommended for  classification problems .
Example: If the dataset has 70% “cats” and 30% “dogs,” then each fold in  StratifiedKFold  will also have approximately 70% cats and 30% dogs.
